# DUMA Driver Safety, Reporting and Fair Review Policy

## Reporting
Any authenticated DUMA user who has interacted with a driver or has credible safety information may submit a confidential report. Capture:
- Driver profile/reference and related trip/load where known.
- Reason category: unsafe driving, fraud, harassment, theft, suspected fake documents, non-delivery, or other.
- Date, time, location and factual description.
- Optional evidence reference (photos, messages, police/case reference where appropriate).
- Whether anyone is in immediate danger.

Do not encourage public accusations, retaliation, threats or disclosure of personal identity documents. Provide an emergency instruction to contact local emergency services/police when there is immediate danger; DUMA reporting is not an emergency response service.

## Review and enforcement
- A report is an allegation, not proof. Never automatically permanently remove or publicly label a driver based only on one report.
- A report is acknowledged and assigned a case/reference number in the backend.
- Restrict access to reports to trained, authorised safety reviewers; keep an immutable audit trail of access and decisions.
- Review evidence, check for duplicate/coordinated/retaliatory reports, and assess severity and credibility.
- For credible immediate safety risks, an authorised reviewer may apply a temporary precautionary restriction on accepting new jobs while urgent review takes place. Record the reason, reviewer and start time; notify the driver where safe and lawful.
- Give the driver a clear summary of the concern and a reasonable opportunity to respond, except where disclosure would create a safety, evidence-tampering or legal risk.
- Decide proportionately: no action, warning, required retraining/document recheck, temporary suspension, or removal for substantiated serious/repeated violations.
- Tell the reporting person the case was reviewed and its outcome category where appropriate, without disclosing the driver's private information.
- Provide an appeal/reconsideration route, independent reviewer where practical, and target response times.
- Escalate credible criminal conduct to appropriate authorities where required or lawful; preserve evidence lawfully.

## Product controls
Report Driver action should be available from a driver profile and relevant Freight trip/load. Reports must be transmitted to a secure central backend, not merely stored on one phone. Store minimal evidence securely, not in public profile fields. Support case status: RECEIVED, TRIAGE, INVESTIGATING, ACTION_TAKEN, CLOSED, APPEAL.
Driver status: PENDING, VERIFIED, RESTRICTED_PENDING_REVIEW, SUSPENDED, REMOVED, APPEAL_PENDING.
Suspended/removed accounts cannot accept loads or create new driver bookings. Existing trips must be handled safely by operations.
The current offline Android prototype stores reports locally only. This is not a shared reporting channel and does not yet provide admin case management, notifications, driver response, appeals or enforceable account restrictions. Those require the secure backend and staff console.

## Privacy and POPIA
Give notice of the purpose and lawful basis for processing reports; collect only relevant factual information; restrict access; protect evidence; define retention/deletion schedules; provide appropriate access/correction mechanisms; and document operator arrangements. Avoid collecting sensitive personal information unless necessary and legally justified. Obtain South African privacy/legal review before launch.
